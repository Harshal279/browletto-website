About Us

Where sweet cravings meet good moods.

At Browletto, every craving deserves something special. From indulgent brownies and waffles to thick shakes, ice cream and coffee, we bring together flavours that make everyday moments sweeter.

We want Browletto to be a welcoming place where friends catch up, families share their favourite treats and every visit brings a little happiness. With 14 outlets and counting, our main office is right here in Jaripatka, Nagpur.

  • Desserts to love Brownies, waffles, ice cream and sweet treats.
  • Something for everyone Shakes, coffee, beverages and snacks.
  • Everyday indulgence Casual hangouts, quick treats and celebrations.
  • A growing brand Expanding our reach and welcoming new partners.

Our vision. To make Browletto a recognised dessert and cafe brand by delivering enjoyable food experiences and creating opportunities for aspiring entrepreneurs to grow with us.
