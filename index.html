About Us

Where sweet cravings meet good moods.

At Browletto, every craving deserves something special. From indulgent brownies and waffles to thick shakes, ice cream and coffee, we bring together flavours that make everyday moments sweeter.

We want Browletto to be a welcoming place where friends catch up, families share their favourite treats and every visit brings a little happiness. With 16 outlets and counting, our main office is in Jaripatka, Nagpur.
